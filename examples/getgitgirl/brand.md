# getgitgirl's reel brand

## Account
Learning to build with AI, shown honestly as I go · women 25-44 who want to use AI properly without the overwhelm

## Reference reel
A creator reel with one-word captions, clean recreated UI cards on screen, and a cut every 2 seconds - what I love: it looks expensive but calm, and every graphic shows exactly what she's saying.

## Colours
- Main: #ED84B0 (hot pink - colour blocks, titles on footage)
- Pop: #FF7DB8 (bubblegum - accent words in captions)
- Light: #FFEEF5 (blush - backgrounds, cards)
- Soft: #EFA6C2 (rose - secondary)
- Never: black or black-and-white text in reels, blue, neon

## Fonts
- Headlines: Brown Bold, lowercase
- Accents: Canela Light italic (the emotional word)
- Captions: Brown Regular
- CapCut stand-in: Work Sans Medium (until Brown is in my CapCut brand kit)

## Captions
One word on screen at a time (2-3 only for a payoff line), big, centred mid-chest, lowercase, white. The key word of each line swaps to Canela italic in pink. No boxes, no pills, no outlines. Over white screens, captions turn hot pink.

## Graphics
Three styles mixed by what the line is doing:
- hook / punchy claim / CTA → editorial headline in the wall space above my head
- one-liner reveal → full hot-pink colour block with my clip framed in it
- describing something → scrapbook page with stickers
- a list → green-screen style, me cut out over a pink page
Cards are clean recreated UI (white, rounded, soft shadow), never phone footage of a monitor. Assets: face stickers, jelly letters, a Barbie-car flipbook for the CTA.

## Sound
Lots, but light: snap on the hook word, whoosh on colour blocks, pop on stickers, typing on terminal shots, coin on the CTA keyword, chime on the last word.

## Pacing
1.1x, cut all fillers, a cut or punch-in every 1.5-2.5 s, under 60 s, never fully silent.

## Call to action
"comment EDIT and I'll send it to you" style keyword CTA, always at the end.

## Rules
- Every reel has at least one carousel (or ask first)
- The hook title is the ONE place outlines are allowed (white outline, big, sits just above my head)
- Cut everything before the camera is turned round, every um, every pause
- Never Inter or Inter Tight
- No outlined or boxed captions
- No black text in reels
- No heavy vignette; true blacks, not washed out
- Graphics must match the exact line being spoken
